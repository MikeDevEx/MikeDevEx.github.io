Privacy Policy for Pompons

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Samoware Games built Pompons (package name com.samoware.Pompons) as a puzzle game that can be played offline. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information we collect

Pompons does not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location, and we do not sell user data. The game does not require an account and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, or social features.

The only data that leaves your device is the technical diagnostics data described below.

Diagnostics data

Pompons is built with the Unity engine. When your device is connected to the internet, Unity's Diagnostics service automatically sends technical data about the game to Unity Technologies, which processes it on our behalf as a service provider. This data includes:

  • crash logs and error reports;
  • app diagnostics and performance data, such as device model, operating system version, app version, and performance measurements;
  • device or other identifiers, such as an installation identifier used to tell game installations apart.

We use this data only for analytics, to find and fix errors and to improve the game's stability and performance. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not shared with other third parties. Collection is part of how the game works and cannot be turned off in the game. Unity keeps diagnostics data for a limited period, 90 days by default. You can read more in Unity's Privacy Policy.

Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress, earned stars, tutorial status, and preferences such as music, sound, and haptics locally on your device. This information is not sent to Samoware Games or to third parties. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Device permissions

Pompons uses the internet permission to send the diagnostics data described above, and may use the device vibration feature to provide optional haptic feedback. Neither gives the game access to your personal information. Haptics can be disabled in the game settings.

Children's privacy

Pompons is designed to be suitable for a broad audience, including children aged 6 and older. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The diagnostics data described above is technical, is collected only to keep the game working correctly, and is not used for advertising.

Security and external links

Diagnostics data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The game does not contain user-generated content, direct messaging, or links that require users to provide personal information.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date above will also be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about the data described here, contact Samoware Games at samowaregames@gmail.com.
