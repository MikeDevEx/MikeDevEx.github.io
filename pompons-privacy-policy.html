Privacy Policy for Pompons

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Samoware Games built Pompons (package name com.samoware.Pompons) as an offline puzzle game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information we collect

Pompons does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or other user data. The game does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, social features, or third-party data collection services.

Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress, earned stars, tutorial status, and preferences such as music, sound, and haptics locally on your device. This information is not sent to Samoware Games or to third parties. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Device permissions

Pompons may use the device vibration feature to provide optional haptic feedback. This does not provide access to personal information. Haptics can be disabled in the game settings.

Children's privacy

Pompons is designed to be suitable for a broad audience, including children aged 6 and older. Because the game does not collect or share user data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and external links

No user data is transmitted to our servers. The game does not contain user-generated content, direct messaging, or links that require users to provide personal information.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date above will also be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Samoware Games at samowaregames@gmail.com.
